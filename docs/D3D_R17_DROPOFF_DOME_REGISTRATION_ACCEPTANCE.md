## Scope and source of the request

The owner supplied a new screenshot of **a blue pickup and white van too close at the Building 1 student drop-off** and explicitly requested **D3D-R17** in the same chat. R17 follows the merged traffic revisions R16–R16D and the locked [D3D-R14–R20 Roadmap](D3D_R14_R20_DEVELOPMENT_ROADMAP.md). ### A. Safer vehicle drop-off spacing

- Added `venues/dropoff-r17.mjs` with a geometry-based separating-axis (oriented vehicle rectangles) guard for moving vehicles in the cross-road zone X=-12..12, Z=16..25.5.
- Preserves actual visual size metadata of modern sedan, passenger van, buses, ten-wheel trucks and the low-poly pickup; the vehicle pair is checked for approximate **0.85 scene-unit body-to-body clearance**, rather than only a 3.6-unit distance between centers.
- A **separate curbside bay** at world coordinates **X=7, Z=18.55** sits **outside the crossed car lane** Z=20..24. New van approach: X=-3,Z22 → X1.6,Z22 → X3.1,Z19.65 → X5.5,Z18.55 → X7,Z18.55; parked orientation parallel to X, then safely rejoins the internal road. Added bay striping, curb and step-off area. Vehicle queues wait if the footprint area is occupied, and release as the lead vehicle departs.
- This prevents the reported photo-like case of a van parked in the through lane while a pickup tries to pass, while allowing non-overlapping cars to pass the physically distinct bay.
- All R16C right-of-way/exit-merge protections, R16D inbound highway approach, and historical road fleet remain unchanged.
